    📋 General CBT Instructions

    1. You need a valid CBT token from your admin/teacher to start any examination — tokens are issued specifically to you and cannot be used by another student.
    2. Each token has its own validity window (e.g. 24 hours) — it must be used to start an exam before it expires.
    3. Once you start, the exam itself also runs on its own countdown — whichever runs out first (the token's validity or the exam duration) will automatically submit your exam.
    4. Each question has exactly one correct answer out of four options (A–D).
    5. Use Save & Next to record your answer and move on, Skip to leave a question unanswered for now, Flag to mark a question for review, and the numbered panel to jump to any question directly.
    6. Your exam is automatically submitted the instant either timer reaches zero, whether or not you've answered every question.
    7. Once submitted, you cannot re-enter that attempt — review your answers from the result screen instead.
    8. Your score and full answer review are available immediately — no waiting for admin approval.
    9. Do not refresh or close the browser tab during an active exam; your progress is saved automatically, but a stable connection gives the smoothest experience.
